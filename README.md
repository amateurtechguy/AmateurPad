# AmateurPad
A 3x3 macropad that lets you get extra shortcuts without having to do any extra work, and also helps you adjust the volume at night without looking for the volume up volume down button with it's rotary encoder!

## What is This?
It is a custom macropad powered by a Seeed XIAO RP2040 microcontroller and QMK Firmware, allowing it to be fully customizable and compact.

Have you ever felt like you need more keys, want a little hack station, or simply just irritated from having to click so many buttons just to open github or your calculator or notes?

Well AmateurPad is for you! Cause with AmateurPad you can write your thoughts down with one click, way before you forget about them quickly!


## Features:

- 3x3 Matrix for 9 beautiful sounding keys
- One Rotary Encoder that you can twist and press to your hearts desire
- Custom PCB to eliminate the need for messy wires
- A 3D printed Case for accessibility and ease
- Custom QMK Firmware which allows easy reprogramming

## Preset Functions:

(My custom firmware comes with these settings included, but feel free to change it to better meet your needs!)

-Rotary Encoder:
Turn Left	Turn Right	Press
Volume Up	Volume Down	Toggle Layers
Keys Base Layer:
Col 1	Col 2	Col 3
Row 1	Ctrl + C	Ctrl + V	Ctrl + X
Row 2	Ctrl + A	Ctrl + F	Ctrl + Z
Row 3	Left Arrow	Up Arrow	Right Arrow
Keys Numpad Layer:
Col 1	Col 2	Col 3
Row 1	7	8	9
Row 2	4	5	6
Row 3	1	2	3


## How To Use:

Download QMK on laptop or pc
then click [here](https://docs.qmk.fm/newbs_flashing) for detailed instructions on how to flash it!
